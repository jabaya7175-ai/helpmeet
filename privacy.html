Privacy

Last updated November 10, 2026

HelpMeet isn't live yet. Right now we only run a waitlist. This page explains, in plain words, what that waitlist collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address
  • Your city, and whether you're joining as a single or as a friend
  • Your church tradition and your church's name, if you choose to tell us. We use your church only to see which churches we're reaching; it's never shown to anyone.
  • Who invited you (if you came through someone's invite link) and which link brought you here

What we do with it

  • Email you about HelpMeet's launch, and nothing else
  • Decide which cities to launch in first
  • Show you how many people joined through your invite link

We never sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Waitlist sign-ups are stored securely with our database provider, Supabase. Only the HelpMeet team can view the list. The website itself can add sign-ups but can't read them.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your sign-up at any time, and every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe. To be removed, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Changes

When the HelpMeet app launches, it will come with a full privacy policy covering profiles, matching and friends. We'll let everyone on the waitlist know before anything changes.
