How it works

Your people, in your corner.

No endless swiping. A few thoughtful matches a day, and the people who know you best helping the date go well.

  1. 1

    Get 7 matches a day

    Thoughtful profiles with church tradition, membership and what matters to you.

  2. 2

    Close friends can help prepare the date

    Your choice of three friends read your match's profile and write questions that bring out your best stories.

  3. 3

    Check in, unlock

    When you both arrive, a code unlocks the questions. Use them, skip them, or just talk.

  4. 4

    Keep going, if you both want

    Having a good time? If you both say yes, your friends' ideas for what to do next unlock right there.

Giving you space to be yourself.

Nerves, small talk, the pressure to impress in the first five minutes. Even the most outgoing people have trouble coming across as themselves on a first date.

Your friends know the stories you'd never bring up yourself, and the side of you that takes a while to show. HelpMeet lets them help, then step aside.

Your church, your call.

  • Only see people from your church, skip them entirely, or let it not matter.
  • Others see only your tradition and city. Your specific church stays private unless you choose to share it.
  • Friends never see how a date went unless you tell them.

Questions

Is HelpMeet free?

Yes, completely. No subscriptions, no paid upgrades, no ads. It's a labor of love for our church community.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Friends are optional. Without them it works like a thoughtful dating app with 7 matches a day. With them, your dates get a little help.

What do my friends see?

A limited version of your match's profile and where and when you're meeting, so their ideas fit nearby. They never see your chats, and never learn how the date went unless you tell them.

Do we have to use the questions?

Not at all. Use one, skip them all, or never open them. They're there in case the conversation needs a nudge.
